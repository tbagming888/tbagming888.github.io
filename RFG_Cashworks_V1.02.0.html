RFG CASHWORKS

This rebuild starts you with almost nothing in a small back-room shop. You have to clean, dry, sort, strap and dispatch dirty cash by hand, then earn enough to buy better tools and machines. The game keeps the simulator-like workflow, but uses original RFG colors, room design and equipment.

Starting setupOld table, wash tub, drying rack, sorting mat and dispatch crate. No free automation.
ProgressionEarn cash → buy bill counter → buy strap press → buy scanner → buy auto line starter.
Color directionLime green, baby blue and pink are the staple colors, with orange and yellow as support accents.
ControlsWASD / drag to look / E interact · On mobile: left joystick, right-side look, USE, RUN.
